What is a trichologist?

A trichologist is a hair and scalp specialist. Instead of a quick look, we read three things under magnification: the strand, the follicle and scalp it grows from, and the body systems behind both, like thyroid, iron and hormones. That read shows what the mirror can't, and it decides which treatment is worth doing.

What we see that others miss

What a 200x read changes about the plan

Six things you can't see in the mirror. Each one changes the plan.

  1. 01

    Whether follicular openings are still present

    If they are, hair can return. If they're gone, it's scarring. Both look like smooth skin to the eye.

  2. 02

    How many hairs come out of each opening

    Healthy openings grow two or three. Dropping to one is early loss, long before your part looks wider.

  3. 03

    Miniaturization

    Thick and thin hairs mixed together. It points to a different cause than even thinning.

  4. 04

    Perifollicular findings

    Redness or scale around the opening. If it's there, we calm the scalp before we stimulate growth.

  5. 05

    The shape of broken hairs

    Tapered or snapped flat? One is a growth problem, the other is breakage.

  6. 06

    A fixed reference

    Same positions every visit, so progress is measured against your own baseline.

Questions, answered

Honest answers before you commit

What is a trichologist?

A trichologist is a specialist in hair and scalp science. The work is reading: the strand itself, the follicle and the scalp it sits in under magnification, and the patterns that point back to what is driving a change. At this practice that read is done at 200x on screen while you watch, and it is paired with a look at the internal picture in the same visit.

What is the difference between a trichologist and a dermatologist?

A dermatologist is a physician covering skin, hair and nails across a wide range of conditions, including prescriptions and procedures. A trichologist focuses only on hair and scalp, and the whole appointment goes into reading them at 200x alongside the systems behind them. Because we work outside insurance, treatment is chosen for what works rather than what is covered. Many of our clients see both.
