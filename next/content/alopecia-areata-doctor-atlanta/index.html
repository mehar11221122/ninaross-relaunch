Active Versus Resolving Alopecia Areata At 200x

An active patch tends to show exclamation-mark hairs at the border, meaning short broken hairs that are narrower at the scalp end than at the tip. Alongside them there are often black dots where a hair broke at the surface and yellow dots at the follicular opening. Follicular openings themselves are preserved, which is the single most important finding, because it means the follicles are still there.

A resolving patch looks different. The border settles, exclamation-mark hairs become scarce, and the field fills with upright regrowing hairs, often fine and lighter in colour than the surrounding hair. Pigment usually returns as the hair matures.

Preserved openings are what separate alopecia areata from a scarring alopecia at the same site, and it is why the read matters before any plan is written. Smooth skin with the openings gone is a different condition with a different answer.

Both states are photographed from the same fixed positions each visit, so at review we compare your patch against your own baseline rather than against an impression of how it looked last time.

Can alopecia areata hair grow back?

In many cases, yes. Alopecia areata preserves the follicle. As long as the openings are intact, which we confirm at 200x, those follicles can produce hair again.

What causes alopecia areata?

Alopecia areata is autoimmune. Your immune system targets your own hair follicles. Common contributing factors include stress, thyroid dysfunction, nutrient deficiencies, gut health issues, and genetic predisposition.

How is alopecia areata different from other hair loss?

Alopecia areata creates smooth, round patches because the immune system targets specific follicles. Traction alopecia comes from tension. CCCA involves scarring. Telogen effluvium is diffuse shedding. Each requires its own approach.

How long does alopecia areata treatment take?

It varies. Some clients may see fine new hairs in 8 to 12 weeks. Fuller regrowth can take 6 to 12 months depending on how long the patch has been present and what the assessment shows. Results not typical. Individual results will vary.

What do I need before coming in?

Just an appointment. Most clients come in with a patch and a question. We assess whether the pattern is alopecia areata, identify the stage, and review potential immune triggers in your first visit.

What happens during the $99 Discovery?

You receive 30 focused minutes with a certified trichologist, a 200x view of your alopecia areata patch on screen, and your written report the next business day.

Can alopecia areata spread?

It can. Some people have a single patch that resolves on its own. Others develop multiple patches or see existing ones expand. Early assessment helps establish the stage and whether follicular openings remain intact.

Do you treat alopecia areata in men?

Yes. We assess scalp patches, beard patches, and eyebrow thinning using the same process: magnification, a wider health review, and a written plan.
