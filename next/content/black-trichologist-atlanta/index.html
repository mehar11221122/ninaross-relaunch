Questions, Answered.

The Things
Women Ask Us Most.

If your question isn't here, call us and ask. You will talk to a real person.

Can my hair actually grow back?

It depends on what is happening at the follicle. Some follicles are dormant and can respond to treatment. Others have been scarred by conditions like advanced CCCA and cannot regrow. That is exactly what the 200x scalp imaging is for. We would rather tell you honestly what is realistic than promise you something we cannot deliver.

How long before I see a difference?

Hair grows slowly, so most plans are measured in months rather than weeks. We photograph the same areas at the same angles so you are not relying on memory to judge whether something changed. Individual results vary.

I have been told there is nothing I can do. Is that true?

Often what that means is that no one looked closely enough. Before you accept that answer, it is worth seeing your scalp at 200x and reviewing what may be happening internally. Sometimes there is more to work with than you were told.

Do I have to take my braids or wig out first?

Come as you are. We will talk through your styling history and work out what we can see at this visit and what may need a follow-up look. Nothing about your hair history is judged here.

What is a trichologist?

A trichologist specializes in the hair and scalp specifically. Our certified trichologists use trichoscopy at 200x and a whole-body evaluation to identify contributing causes.
