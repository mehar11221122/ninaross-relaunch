How PRP Hair Treatment Works

Your Own Blood. Your Own Growth Factors.
Placed Where Your Follicles Can Use Them.

PRP stands for platelet-rich plasma. A small amount of your blood is drawn, spun to separate the platelet-rich portion, and the concentrated growth factors are placed directly into the scalp where your 200x read identified viable follicles.

01 Draw A small volume of your blood is drawn in the clinic, using the same process as a routine blood draw.
02 Process Your blood is spun to separate and concentrate the platelet-rich plasma, where the growth factors sit.
03 Deliver The PRP concentrate is placed across the mapped treatment areas at the depth and spacing determined by your 200x read.

When A Different Path
Comes First

These findings tell us PRP is premature. A different step comes first, and that step is handled here.

✕ Trichoscopy shows no viable follicles in the target area. Where the follicular openings are gone, there is nothing left to support and PRP is not offered for that area.
✕ Advanced scarring alopecia with an active inflammatory border. The inflammation is addressed first. Treating over an active scarring process spends your money on tissue that is still being lost.
✕ A shedding pattern that points to an internal driver we have not sorted yet, such as thyroid, iron, or a recent medication change. Growth signaling into a scalp that is shedding for a systemic reason is the wrong first step, so the internal work comes first.
✕ Active infection, open lesions, or untreated inflammatory scalp disease at the site. The scalp is settled before anything is placed into it.
✕ Blood or platelet conditions and medications that make a draw or a platelet concentrate inappropriate. This is reviewed in your history at the Discovery.
✕ Expectations that the read cannot support, for example a fully bare area a client wants restored to teenage density. We say so plainly and talk about what is realistic, including whether cosmetic density is a better fit.

When PRP hair treatment is premature, the alternative path is handled here: settling inflammation, addressing an internal driver, or exploring cosmetic density. One team, one location.

What is PRP hair treatment?

PRP stands for platelet-rich plasma. A small amount of your blood is drawn, spun to concentrate the growth factors, and placed directly into the scalp where follicles are underperforming. Your own biology supplies the treatment material.

Does PRP hair treatment actually work?

It can support follicles that are still alive. That is why we confirm candidacy at 200x before recommending a series. Where follicle openings are preserved, PRP delivers growth factors that support dormant and miniaturizing follicles.

How many PRP sessions will I need?

A typical PRP hair treatment series may involve 3 to 4 sessions spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart, followed by maintenance. Your 200x read and response over time determine the plan for you.

How long until I see results from PRP hair treatment?

Some clients begin noticing changes within 8 to 12 weeks, while fuller development may take 6 to 12 months. We photograph fixed positions every visit so progress can be measured. Individual timelines and results vary.

Does PRP hair treatment hurt?

Most clients describe pressure and brief stinging during delivery. The scalp may feel tender afterward for a day or two. Comfort measures are used, and you can ask us to pause at any point.

Why do you pair PRP with microneedling?

Microneedling creates controlled micro-channels that help the PRP concentrate reach the depth where follicles sit. It also triggers the scalp's own repair signaling, so the two treatments work through complementary pathways.

Can PRP hair treatment help with traction alopecia?

It may help when traction alopecia is caught before scarring closes the follicle openings. If the openings at your edges remain visible at 200x, PRP may support those follicles. Where openings are gone, a different path is recommended.

Is PRP hair treatment safe for Black hair?

PRP uses material prepared from your own blood. Our certified trichologists adjust depth, spacing, and aftercare for textured hair and your scalp findings. Your health history is reviewed before treatment.

We confirm candidacy at 200x magnification before recommending a single session. You see your follicles on screen and learn whether PRP has viable tissue to support before you invest in a series.
